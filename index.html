The Final KPI Formula
Final KPI % =
(Portfolio % × Portfolio wt)
+ (Planning % × Planning wt)
+ (Submission % × Submission wt)
+ (Monthly Progress % × Monthly wt)
+ (Adhoc % × Adhoc wt)
− Late Penalty
The same five components make up the Weekly, Monthly and YTD Average scores — only the time window and the weights differ.

What each component means
Portfolio %Share of that staff member's active, Medium-priority weekly (or monthly) tasks that were touched at least once in the period — any status (Completed / In Progress / Pending) counts as touched.
Planning %Of the days a "planned task for tomorrow" was logged, how often that planned item actually showed up as done the next working day.
Submission %Days submitted ÷ expected working days (Tue–Fri). Approved leave days are removed from the expected count, so leave is never penalised.
Monthly Progress %Share of active monthly tasks touched at least once so far in the current month.
Adhoc %MIN(adhoc items logged ÷ 5, 100%) — adhoc checkbox ticks plus distinct items named in free text.
Late Penalty0.5% deducted per submission logged after 7:30pm.

Where the "Reason" / variance text comes from
Every score on the Weekly, Monthly and YTD Avg tabs comes with a plain-English explanation, generated automatically from the same components above — not a separate judgment call:
Flagged lowPortfolio < 60%, Submission < 80%, or Monthly Progress < 40%
Flagged strongPortfolio ≥ 90%
Adhoc called outHeavy load if Adhoc % ≥ 75% (i.e. 4+ items/week); "no adhoc logged" if zero
Late submissionsNamed whenever any late-penalty days exist in the period
Month-over-month (Monthly tab only)"Improved" / "Dropped" shown when the final score moves ±5% or more vs the prior month
YTD Average (May to date)
The Weekly average is the mean of every completed week's Final KPI % so far this year; the Monthly average is the mean of every completed month's Final Monthly KPI %. Both are bounded to the calendar year — once a year closes, that year's number is final, and the next January starts counting from zero again.
